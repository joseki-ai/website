About Joseki

Joseki builds infrastructure for quantitative trading research: market data, feature engineering, simulation, machine learning and workflow tooling.

Origin

Joseki began as the research and trading infrastructure built for our own strategies. The components were generalized into products for teams that build the same pipeline: data capture, feature computation, a realistic simulator and machine-learning infrastructure.

Team

Joseki is built by a small team, each member an expert in their own area, who care about the work and improve the platform continuously.

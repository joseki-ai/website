  • Market data for the requested venues. Full-depth history with nanosecond timestamps, read in place by the simulator and models.
  • Simulation at the required resolution. From full order book replay to daily bars, with configurable market models.
  • Managed machine learning. GPU training, hyperparameter search and reinforcement-learning environments.
  • Direct contact with the engineering team. Pilot requirements shape the release order.

Products · Market Data

Integrated market data

Raw and full-depth market data with nanosecond hardware timestamps, retained in full history and read in place by strategies, features, simulations and models. Nothing to download, no storage to manage.

Request early access → What is captured
Capture

Colocated capture with nanosecond timestamps

Feeds are captured in colocation facilities from direct exchange feeds, on FPGA and hardware-timestamping network adapters with kernel bypass, synchronized against a GPS clock source over PTP. Every record carries the matching engine timestamp and the receive timestamp, with sequence numbers preserved.

  • Event timestamp. The time the event was received by the matching engine, at the venue's own resolution.
  • Receive timestamp. Taken in hardware at capture: nanosecond resolution, sub-microsecond accuracy to UTC, monotonic for each instrument.
  • Raw messages retained. Every message in the venue's native protocol, lossless, alongside the decoded books.
  • FPGA capture
  • hardware timestamping
  • PTP · IEEE 1588
  • GPS clock source
  • nanosecond resolution
  • kernel bypass
  • UTC, leap-second aware
Data

Raw messages to daily bars

Data is stored as captured. Order books, trades and bars are decoded from the raw messages. Normalization, resampling and alignment are optional transforms, applied at the feature stage when a model needs them.

Integration

Read in place by strategies, simulations and models

Strategies, feature computations, simulations and training jobs read the data where it is stored. There is no download, no copy to maintain and no storage to provision. History is retained and versioned on the platform.

  • Fast to load. Columnar storage indexed by instrument and time, located next to the simulators and GPUs, so a backtest starts without a data transfer.
  • One copy. Every product reads the same dataset version, so results agree across research, backtests and models.
History and coverage

Long history across venues and asset classes

Full-depth history is retained from the day a venue is added, with no roll-off. Equities, futures, options, FX and event contracts; each dataset lists its venues, record types and available date range.

Data quality

Explicit quality flags and versioned datasets

Records affected by an unrecoverable gap or an unreliable receive timestamp are flagged. Nothing is interpolated.

  • Gap flags. A flag on every record affected by a gap in the channel.
  • Point-in-time reference data. Instrument definitions, symbology and corporate actions as of each date, with no look-ahead.
  • Versioned datasets. A published dataset version is immutable; corrections are released as new versions.
  • corporate actions
  • survivorship-bias-free universes
  • continuous futures and rolls
  • point-in-time symbology
